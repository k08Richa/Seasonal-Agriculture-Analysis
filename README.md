# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Overview
Exploratory data analysis of 4,000 farm records from across India, 
completed as the major DIY project for the VOIS for Tech Data Analytics 
Program by Vodafone Idea Foundation and Edunet Foundation (AICTE collaborative).

## Objective
- To analyze agricultural data from different seasons 
and identify meaningful patterns, trends, relationships and differences in agricultural 
performance.
- To investigate how agricultural performance in crop yield, profitability, 
water usage, and disease risk varies across Indian farming 
seasons: Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid.

## Tools Used
- Python (pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn)
- Microsoft Excel (initial data exploration)
- Google Colab

## Dataset
- 4,000 farm records | 28 variables
- Covers: crop type, season, state, rainfall, temperature, soil 
  properties, irrigation method, fertilizer, pesticide, seed quality, 
  revenue, cost, profit, water usage, disease/pest risk

## Key Findings
- Kharif is the most productive and profitable season
- Zaid (summer) season records average losses
- Sugarcane has significantly higher yield than all other crops
- Sugarcane and Chilli are the only crops with consistently 
  positive average profit
- Rainfed farming shows highest water efficiency ratio, 
  Drip is most efficient among managed irrigation methods
- Disease and pest risk is normally distributed (35-60%) 
  across all farms, a universal concern and not region-specific
